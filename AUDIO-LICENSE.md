# Bundled human pronunciation audio

Source: Basic Tibetan, Jampa Khedup and Sonam Tsering (2026), Michigan State University Libraries.

- Alphabet: https://openbooks.lib.msu.edu/basictibetan/chapter/1-1/
- Vowels: https://openbooks.lib.msu.edu/basictibetan/chapter/1-3/
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/

Changes: converted AAC recordings to PCM WAV; alphabet resampled to 24 kHz mono; thirty individual letter clips and eight row clips split at silent boundaries in the published alphabet order. Original voice, pitch and reading order retained. These are human recordings, not synthesized speech. The individual clips inherit the same CC BY-NC 4.0 license. The application is a noncommercial educational project.

The recording dialect has not been independently certified as native Lhasa. Use alongside the app’s Lhasa-oriented phonetic guides and obtain qualified teacher feedback for accent work.

Four vowel clips isolate the i/u/e/o entries after the spoken introduction, retaining their textbook order.
